## Prompt (inglese)
Use the attached Implast crest as the only logo reference: reproduce it faithfully, without redrawing, restyling or adding details, and do not invent any other logo.

Scene: a night amateur football pitch under floodlights. A single white football rests on the penalty spot in the foreground, sharp and lit like a full stop. Behind it, slightly out of focus, a tall navy flag hangs from a pole planted at the top of a short staircase of three navy steps. The attached Implast crest is printed on the flag. The two lower steps are empty, each just one step below the top. The image should read as "one point ahead, standing at the head".

Light and mood: cold floodlight from above, a light mist over the grass, deep shadows, calm and confident rather than celebratory.

Framing: horizontal 2:1, low camera angle from the ball. The ball sits in the lower third and the flag on the right third. Keep the upper left half as clean dark sky for the headline.

Palette: dominant deep navy blue (#0A2342) and white, with one cyan accent (#00A8E8) in the floodlight glow and on the edges of the steps. No other colours except the crest's original colours.

Avoid: any text, letters or numbers; invented logos or badges; players, people or faces; hands; trophies; extra balls; distorted geometry.

Behind it, slightly out of focus, stand three separate square navy blocks in a row, like a podium. The centre block is exactly one step taller than the two side blocks, which are the same height as each other. On each side block there is a bare flagpole with no flag, so the two chasers are clearly waiting just one step below. The image should read as "one point ahead, two rivals one step behind".

Framing: horizontal 2:1, low camera angle from the ball. The ball sits in the lower third, left of centre; the three blocks sit on the right third. Keep the whole upper left half as clean dark sky for the headline.

Palette: dominant deep navy blue (#0A2342) and white, with one cyan accent (#00A8E8) as a thin glow on the top edges of the three blocks and in the floodlight haze. No other colours except the crest's original colours.
