## Prompt (inglese)

Use the two attached official club crests as exact references: the Uso Borgonato crest and the Implast dragon crest. Reproduce both faithfully, with their original shapes and colours, and do not redraw, simplify or invent any detail.

Scene: night amateur football pitch under floodlights. The two crests stand face to face on the halfway line, the same size and at the same height, like two shields in perfect balance. Between them lies a match ball, exactly on the centre spot. Neither side is ahead: the composition is perfectly symmetrical, a draw made visible.

Light and atmosphere: cold white floodlight from above, a light mist over the wet grass, sharp reflections on the crests and a tense, suspended mood at the end of the match.

Framing and format: horizontal 2:1, low camera at pitch level, wide shot. Keep the top third clean and dark (night sky and floodlight haze) to leave room for the headline.

Palette: dominant deep navy blue and white, with a single accent of bright cyan (#00A8E8) in the floodlight glow or the pitch lines. Apart from the crests' own original colours, add no other colours.

Avoid: any text, letters, numbers or scoreboards; invented or altered logos; extra crests; people, faces or hands; cartoon style; heavy lens flare.
